Battery Storage

Store it now, use it later.

Home battery storage that banks your solar and off-peak energy — ready for when you actually need it.

Overview

Get more from every unit.

A home battery stores the electricity your solar panels generate during the day — or cheap off-peak energy overnight — so you can use it in the evening when you'd otherwise be buying at peak rates.

It's the key to getting real value from solar, and it can keep essential circuits running during a power cut too.

We size and install the right battery for your home and usage, and integrate it neatly with your solar and heating systems.
