Who we are

One team for the whole climate of your home.

Scarlatt Climate Control keeps your home at the perfect temperature all year round — warm in winter, cool in summer, and running on cleaner energy. From traditional boilers to the latest heat pumps, solar and battery storage, we design, supply, install and maintain it all.

Whether you're replacing a tired old boiler, adding air conditioning, or making the switch to low-carbon heating, you get honest advice, quality kit from market-leading manufacturers, and workmanship we stand behind.

  • Heating & cooling under one roof — no juggling multiple trades.
  • Efficiency first — systems specced to cut your bills and carbon.
  • Looked after — servicing and maintenance to keep it all running.
