About us

Your home's climate, in expert hands.

Who we are

Heating and renewables specialists.

Scarlatt Climate Control looks after every part of a home's climate — from traditional gas and oil boilers to the newest low-carbon technology. We supply and install air conditioning, air and ground source heat pumps, solar PV and battery storage, and we maintain it all afterwards.

Our approach is simple: survey properly, recommend honestly, install to a high standard, and be there for the long term. Whether you want to cut your bills, lower your carbon footprint, or simply have a home that's comfortable all year, we make it straightforward.
