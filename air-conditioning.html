Air Conditioning

Cool in summer, warm in winter.

Wall-mounted and multi-split air conditioning for homes and offices — supplied, installed and serviced.

Overview

Perfect temperature, all year round.

Modern air conditioning does far more than cool a room on a hot day. Today's systems are highly efficient heat pumps in their own right — so they'll take the edge off a summer heatwave and gently warm a room in winter too.

We install discreet wall-mounted units for a single room, or multi-split systems that condition several rooms from one outdoor unit — ideal for homes, offices and workspaces.

We supply, install, service and maintain, so your system stays clean, efficient and reliable.
